Frequently Asked Questions

Understanding FII & DII Cash Market Flows

Everything you need to know about foreign and domestic institutional money movements, reporting timings, and data analysis.

QWhy does FII / DII activity matter for Indian stock markets?

Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) manage massive global capital pools. Their aggressive buying or selling heavily steers the direction of benchmark indices like Nifty 50 and Sensex. Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) provide critical counter-balancing domestic liquidity to stabilize market drawdowns.

QWhat time is daily FII DII data published by NSE?

Provisional FII and DII cash market trading figures are published daily on stock exchange portals (NSE & BSE) between 5:30 PM and 6:30 PM IST on every trading day.

QWhat is the difference between Gross Buy, Gross Sell, and Net Flow?

Gross Buy represents the total rupee value of shares purchased, and Gross Sell represents total shares sold during normal market hours. Net Flow is Gross Buy minus Gross Sell: positive indicates net capital injection (buying pressure), negative indicates capital outflow.

QCan retail investors follow FII DII flows for trading strategies?

Yes. Consistent multi-day FII buying often fuels sustained index rallies and currency appreciation, while consecutive heavy FII selling accompanied by weak DII support often signals distribution phases and downside volatility.
